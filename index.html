Model Assumptions and Validation

Crop evapotranspiration ETc = Kc × ETo. Gross irrigation depth = ETc / application efficiency, assuming negligible effective rainfall and no soil-water storage credit. The daily irrigation volume is delivered during the specified operating hours. Pressure head is specified at the field inlet; additional emitter distribution losses are not separately resolved. Pipe friction uses Darcy–Weisbach with a Reynolds-number-dependent friction factor (64/Re for laminar; Swamee–Jain approximation for turbulent). Minor losses are modeled as K × v²/(2g). Pump shaft power = ρgQH/η. Electrical energy assumes motor efficiency of 90%. Elevation head may be positive or negative; this educational tool constrains total pump head to a nonnegative value. Real systems require emitter uniformity, network topology, pump curves, suction conditions, rainfall, soil storage, and pressure regulation checks.
